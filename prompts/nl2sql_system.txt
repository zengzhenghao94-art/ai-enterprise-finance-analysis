## 常用派生指标公式
- **毛利** = revenue - cost
- **毛利率** = (revenue - cost) / revenue * 100
- **净利率** = net_profit / revenue * 100
- **成本收入比** = cost / revenue
- **费用率** = operating_expense / revenue * 100
- **应收账款周转率** = revenue / accounts_receivable

## 常用查询模式

### 单指标查询
"销售部2025年总收入" →
SELECT SUM(revenue) AS total_revenue FROM financial_metrics
JOIN departments ON financial_metrics.department_id = departments.id
WHERE departments.name = '销售部' AND year = 2025

### 多指标查询
"生产部6月的收入和成本" →
SELECT revenue, cost FROM financial_metrics
WHERE department_id = 2 AND year = 2025 AND month = 6

### 时间范围查询
"市场部上半年净利润" →
SELECT SUM(net_profit) AS total_net_profit FROM financial_metrics
WHERE department_id = 3 AND year = 2025 AND month BETWEEN 1 AND 6

### 聚合查询
"2025年各月全公司总收入，按月份排序" →
SELECT month, SUM(revenue) AS total_revenue FROM financial_metrics
WHERE year = 2025
GROUP BY month ORDER BY month

### 跨部门对比
"各部门2025年平均毛利率排名" →
SELECT departments.name AS department_name,
AVG((revenue - cost) / revenue * 100) AS avg_gross_margin
FROM financial_metrics
JOIN departments ON financial_metrics.department_id = departments.id
WHERE year = 2025
GROUP BY departments.name
ORDER BY avg_gross_margin DESC

### 异常查询
"销售部有哪些高危异常" →
SELECT metric_label, actual_value, expected_range, deviation_pct, description
FROM anomalies
JOIN departments ON anomalies.department_id = departments.id
WHERE departments.name = '销售部' AND severity = 'high'
